# Telegram Gemini Bot films: V4

Owner-approved 20-second product films, separately composed for 1920x1080 and 1080x1920 at 60 fps. The vertical cut has its own layout and safe areas. Four illustrative phone forms carry the conversation through stock, Bitcoin, gold and /brief examples.

The interface and phone shells are staged film graphics. Response excerpts come from the actual bot runtime: NVDA and Gold in [film-v4-evidence.json](film-v4-evidence.json), and retained Bitcoin and /brief output in [demo-evidence.json](demo-evidence.json). Quotes are historical, and editing shortens processing time. No personal chats, account identifiers, credentials or calendar dates appear on screen. Device artwork is illustrative, not vendor CAD or a device-compatibility claim.

Only the synchronized finished films are redistributed here. Chimes, taps, scroll ticks and the restrained peak accents are original local synthesis. No narration or synthetic voice. The owner approved the revised final picture and sound before publication.

Both exports passed full decode, exact dimensions/duration/frame count, H.264 High/yuv420p, limited BT.709, fast-start MP4, stereo AAC at 48 kHz and audio checks. Each is 20 seconds and 1,200 frames. Encoded loudness is -14.01 LUFS, true peak -4.92 dBTP. File sizes and SHA256 hashes: [film-v4-manifest.json](film-v4-manifest.json). Separate posters accompany both films. Earlier films remain preserved in Git history and the original film workspace.
